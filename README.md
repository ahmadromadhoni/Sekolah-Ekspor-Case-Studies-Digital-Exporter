# Sekolah Ekspor — Export Case Studies & Weekly Assignments

Portfolio of practical assignments, export trade documentation, market analysis, risk mitigation, financing comparison, and logistics planning completed during the **PELURU UT x Sekolah Ekspor** program.

## Structure Overview
- `01-Identifikasi-Potensi-Produk-Lokal` — Local export product potential research presentation.
- `02-Pengembangan-Media-Promosi` — Export product brochure design.
- `03-Identitas-Korporasi-Eksportir` — Exporter brand identity and business card design.
- `04-Analisis-Mitigasi-Risiko-Ekspor` — Export challenges and risk mitigation analysis.
- `05-Administrasi-Pengiriman-Packing-List` — Export packing list documentation.
- `06-Pembiayaan-Perdagangan-LC` — Letter of Credit (L/C) banking facility comparison report.
- `07-Strategi-Rantai-Pasok-Forwarder` — Freight forwarder and shipping route mapping report.
- `08-Optimasi-Moda-Transportasi-Loading-Plan` — Transport mode, container specification, and loading plan report.
